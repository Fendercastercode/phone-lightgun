How to play

THREE STEPS TO THE FIRST SHOT

The phone's camera tracks the room, not the TV. RetroSight knows where you are and where the screen is, so the aim holds when you move around.

  1. PAIR BY QR CODE

    Run the free PC app on Linux or a Raspberry Pi 5 (Windows coming soon). Scan the code with the phone. It finds your emulators and sets them up for the gun.

  2. MEASURE YOUR TV

    Sweep the phone across the markers on screen once. The PC works out the TV's size and where it sits in your room, then remembers the spot.

  3. LOCK ON AND SHOOT

    Point at the TV for about a second to lock on, take 5 shots at the targets, and play. Shoot off-screen or flick the phone to reload.

WHAT'S IN THE MACHINE

Everything below is in the app today, on the phone and the PC.

AIM

ABSOLUTE AIM, ANY FLAT TV

Point at the spot and that's where the shot lands. Works on TVs and monitors of any size, including big living-room screens.

AIM

FINDS THE TV AGAIN

Saved locations per room. Pick the phone back up and it recognises the room and puts the aim back on the locked spot.

AIM

STEADY SHOTS

Built-in shot processing keeps every shot on target, so it lands where you meant it to.

SETUP

EMULATORS SET UP FOR YOU

The PC app writes each emulator's own gun settings, with a backup and one-tap undo. RetroArch, MAME, DuckStation, PCSX2, Dolphin and more.

SETUP

ROOM CHECK

Too dark, or nothing for the camera to hold on to? It tells you why and what to try, then lets you carry on anyway. Printable tracking sheets for plain walls.

SETUP

LAST-SESSION REPORT

After a game the PC app explains what went wrong, if anything, and how to fix it: lighting, Wi-Fi, tracking drops.

FEEL

RELOAD LIKE A LIGHT GUN

Shoot off-screen to reload, or flick the phone. The flick sensitivity is yours to tune.

FEEL

SOUNDS AND RECOIL

Gun, zap, phaser or retro sound sets, picked to suit the game. A recoil buzz on every shot, with strength you can set.

FEEL

SIGHTS ON THE PHONE

A sight mark on the edge of the phone's screen gives you the same aiming reference every shot, for games without their own crosshair.

PLAY

TWO PLAYERS

A friend joins with their own phone: two guns at once in MAME.

PLAY

TARGET PRACTICE

A shooting gallery with a score and a rank. It fine-tunes your aim from where your shots land.

PLAY

PC REMOTE

Start games from the phone, and use it as the PC's touchpad and keyboard from the couch.
